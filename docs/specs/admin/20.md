## Data mapping (src/db/schema.ts). Period = [from,to) in Asia/Tbilisi; campaign = selected `challenges.id`.
- **ნახვები (views)**: `SELECT SUM((sp.metrics->>'views')::int), COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE sp.metrics IS NOT NULL) , MAX(sp.last_synced_at)` over `social_posts sp JOIN missions m ON m.id=sp.mission_id WHERE m.challenge_id=:c AND sp.published_at in range AND sp.status IN ('verified','metrics_unavailable'...)`. Metrics are admin-entered (`metricsSource`, e.g. "admin"). If no row has metrics -> show `მიუწვდომელია` ("unavailable"), NOT 0. Always show caption "წყარო: {metricsSource} · {lastSyncedAt}" (replace the fake `არჩევის პერიოდიდან`). Only posts with non-null metrics are summed; show "N/M posts" coverage.
- **მონაწილეები**: `SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT user_id) FROM participations WHERE challenge_id=:c AND joined_at in range`.
- **ნამუშევრები მიღებული**: `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM submissions s JOIN missions m ON m.id=s.mission_id WHERE m.challenge_id=:c AND s.status='approved' AND s.reviewed_at in range` (period basis reviewed_at vs created_at is an open decision).
- **QR ვიზიტები დადასტურებული**: `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM checkins c JOIN qr_codes q ON q.id=c.qr_code_id JOIN missions m ON m.id=q.mission_id WHERE m.challenge_id=:c AND c.created_at in range` (unique per user+qr). Applies only to `challenges.kind='visit'`; for kind 'video' show unavailable/"არ ეხება".
- **მონაწილეობა დროში chart**: `SELECT date_bin/ date_trunc('day', joined_at AT TIME ZONE 'Asia/Tbilisi') AS bucket, COUNT(*) FROM participations WHERE challenge_id=:c AND joined_at in range GROUP BY 1` (bucket into 12 equal slices for 30d; 1 per day for 7d).
- **Funnel**: views (social_posts, may be unavailable) -> participations -> QR check-ins. If views unavailable, the funnel must start at participations and omit step 1 (no fabricated 100%). Note: step ratios mix different entities (posts views vs users vs scans), label as "ნაბიჯები" not as true conversion.
- No data source: none of the 4 cards is impossible, but `ნახვები` depends on manual admin entry; no reach/impressions/followers/demographics anywhere on this screen.
