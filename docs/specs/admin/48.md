## Mapping to the data model (what exists / what does not)
Three categories x four weights. Existing data:
- Creative (30%): human judgement; the only data is `submissions` with status `approved` (+ `reviewNote`). No score field.
- Audience / "real views" (25% engagement + 20% views): `social_posts.metrics {views,likes,comments}` (nullable, unknown ≠ 0), `metricsSource`, `lastSyncedAt`, `status` (verified | metrics_unavailable | refresh_failed | unavailable...). The rule "დაუზუსტებელი მონაცემები საჭიროებს გადამოწმებას" ⇒ candidates whose metrics are null / status not `verified` must be flagged for manual verification and must NOT be ranked as 0.
- Business (25%): "confirmed customers or sales" — NO data model (only `checkins` per QR code = visits, and `vouchers.status="redeemed"` per reward could be a proxy; neither equals sales). Needs a new source (admin-entered business result) or must be shown as "unavailable".
- Prize: `rewards` (titleKa, valueGel, challengeId, stock) and `vouchers` (issued to a user) cover the "product/service" prize; "cash" and "combined" prizes have no field.
- Challenge levels (`challenge_levels`) are XP progress tiers — NOT winner ranks; do not reuse them for winners.
NOT in the model (propose a `challenge_winners` table): challengeId, category enum (creative|audience|business), userId, submissionId, score components (creativity, engagement, views, business), rewardId, confirmedBy, confirmedAt, plus history in `audit_log` (exists: actorId, action, target, data). Also undefined: tie handling, repeat-winner rules, appeals (49 says they "must be defined before publication").
